Jan Van Dam Publisher — Privacy Policy

Effective 31 Aug 2026 Applies to: TikTok, Instagram & YouTube integrations

Jan Van Dam Publisher is a single-purpose internal tool: it publishes pre-produced short-form video news clips to a small set of social accounts that its operator personally owns and has explicitly connected. It is not a public app, does not accept sign-ups, and does not collect data from anyone other than the connected account owner.

What the tool does

Jan Van Dam Publisher authenticates with TikTok (and equivalent Instagram/YouTube APIs) using the standard OAuth login flow, then uploads or posts a video file, caption, and cover image that the operator has already produced. It performs no other actions on the connected accounts.

Information we access

  • Basic profile info (via the user.info.basic scope) — open ID, display name, and avatar, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Video publishing permission (via the video.upload scope) — used solely to upload the video files the operator provides.

We do not access followers, direct messages, watch history, analytics, or any data beyond what is listed above.

Information we do not collect

The tool has no user accounts, no sign-up form, no analytics tracking, and no advertising integration. It never sees or stores information about anyone who later views the published content.

How access tokens are stored

OAuth access tokens are stored locally on the operator's own machine, are never transmitted to any third party, and are used exclusively to publish the content described above. A token can be revoked at any time from the connected account's own app-permissions settings, which immediately stops all access.

Data retention & deletion

No personal data is retained beyond the access token itself. Revoking the app's permission on the platform side removes our ability to access the account; the operator can additionally delete the stored token directly.

Changes to this policy

If this tool's scope of access ever changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change accordingly.
